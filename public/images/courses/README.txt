1. /Courses/

2. /Services/
   Contains individual subfolders for each of the 6 Other Services We Offer:
   - stage_shows/
   - wedding_choreography/
   - workshops_1day/
   - workshops_3day/
   - competition_choreography/
   - corporate_events/

Inside each subfolder:
- main.jpg     -> The primary card display photo
- slide_01.jpg -> Slideshow photo 1
- slide_02.jpg -> Slideshow photo 2
- slide_03.jpg -> Slideshow photo 3

HOW TO UPDATE / CHANGE PHOTOS:
Simply replace 'main.jpg' (or any slide_*.jpg) inside that specific course or service folder!
