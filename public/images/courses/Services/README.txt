OTHER SERVICES WE OFFER — PHOTO SUBFOLDERS
=============================================================================

SUBFOLDERS:
- stage_shows/                 -> Event-Based Stage Show Choreography
- wedding_choreography/        -> Wedding Choreography
- workshops_1day/              -> One-Day Dance Workshops
- workshops_3day/              -> Three-Day Dance Workshops
- competition_choreography/    -> Competition-Based Choreography
- corporate_events/            -> Corporate Event Stage Shows

INSIDE EACH SUBFOLDER:
- main.jpg     : Primary card thumbnail and header photo
- slide_01.jpg : Live slideshow photo 1
- slide_02.jpg : Live slideshow photo 2
- slide_03.jpg : Live slideshow photo 3

HOW TO UPDATE:
Drop your new photo directly into the respective service subfolder replacing 'main.jpg'
or any of the 'slide_*.jpg' files. Zero cropping is applied automatically!
